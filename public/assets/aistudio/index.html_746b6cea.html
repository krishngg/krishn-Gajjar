About us

A Gujarati table, kept warm for generations

At Gir Kathiyavadi, we serve our signature Gujarati Thali — robust, honest and full of character. Our kitchen grinds its own masalas, roasts its own jeera and simmers dal slowly.

The thali is a complete experience: seasonal sabzi, dal, kadhi, rice, fresh rotli/rotla, papad, pickle and a glass of chilled chaas.

We are a pure vegetarian, family-friendly restaurant in Balapir, Kadi — with clean, comfortable seating and genuine Gujarati hospitality.

  • Fresh vegetables cooked the slow, traditional way
  • Pure vegetarian kitchen
  • Unlimited thali refills
  • Warm Gujarati hospitality
